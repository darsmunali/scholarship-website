Application Guide

A simple step-by-step guide to applying for scholarships

Six steps

Steps to Apply

Click a step to read more

  1. 1

    Start early – ideally 6 to 12 months before the deadline.

    • Use the Scholarship Directory to find options for your level
    • Run the Eligibility Checker to narrow down the list
    • Note each deadline in a calendar
    6 to 12 months before
  2. 2

    Read the official criteria line by line. Check CGPA, income limits, age limits, nationality and field of study before you spend time on an application.

    1 to 2 days per scholarship
  3. 3

    Collect attested copies of your transcripts, CNIC, income certificate and other documents. Scan them clearly as PDFs and name the files properly.

    2 to 4 weeks
  4. 4
    • Explain your goals and why this scholarship matters to you
    • Give real examples instead of general claims
    • Stay within the word limit and proofread twice
    1 to 2 weeks
  5. 5

    Ask teachers or employers who know your work well. Give them at least three weeks and share your CV and the scholarship details with them.

    At least 3 weeks
  6. 6

    Submit a few days before the deadline to avoid last-minute problems. Save your confirmation email and prepare for an interview if one is part of the process.

    A few days before the deadline

Tips for a Strong Application

Apply to several scholarships.

Competition is high, so do not depend on just one.

Tailor every essay.

Each scholarship has different goals – write to match them.

Keep a document folder.

Store scanned copies so you can reuse them quickly.

Avoid fake offers.

Real scholarships never ask you to pay to apply.
